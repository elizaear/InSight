About

Why this site exists

Ingredient lists are written for regulators, not for people. This site translates them into plain English so you can make your own informed choices, without the scare stories or the marketing spin.

Where our information comes from

We rely on regulators and scientific bodies such as Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ), the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), the Australian Industrial Chemicals Introduction Scheme (AICIS), the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the World Health Organization. Every ingredient page lists its sources.

Food additive numbers

In Australia and New Zealand, food additives are listed by number (for example 621 for MSG). Europe uses the same numbers with an “E” in front (E621). You can search for either.

Important

This site is general information only. It isn't medical, legal or professional advice. If you have an allergy, a health condition, or are pregnant, speak to a doctor or pharmacist. Always follow the directions on product labels.
